The house Masarykova · Zagreb

„beštija“

besh-tee-yah

Beast — in the old tongue, a wild one. The name we cook under.

Casual bistro, serious cooking. Beštija grew out of market mornings at Dolac and nights in the courtyard off Masarykova — a small crew cooking what the day brings.

The crew

Chef Mateo Davidović and the floor team keep the room moving: sharp plates, Croatian wine by the glass, and a house that still feels like a neighbourhood bistro.

The room

Inside, wood tables and bentwood chairs. Outside, the courtyard — tucked away from the street, known to anyone who celebrates food. Lunch through late dinner, Tuesday to Sunday.

The kitchen

Adriatic fish, market vegetables, and a menu that shifts with the week. No theatre — just clean cooking, good drinks, and a table worth lingering at.
