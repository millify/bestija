Our story

Scroll
Masarykova · Zagreb

A private oasis in the heart of Zagreb — in the yard of one of the main streets, still a known address to everyone who celebrates food, and a refuge for those yet to discover it.

The house

The best menus are arranged by the season, especially when the magic happens under the auspices of a small team with big ideas and precise execution. Our menus are smooth, tasty, playful and loose with a clear guiding thread: seasonal food that is the queen of every plate.

The crew

In Beštija, our team is made up of talented individuals who work together to create an exceptional dining experience. From the chef to the waiters, each member plays an integral role in ensuring that every guest leaves satisfied and eager to return.

The kitchen

Our menu changes with each season. It is our mission to offer guests a different version of a familiar, traditional dish — and delight them at the same time.

Our philosophy

Every day we want as many satisfied gourmands who return again and again — as many empty plates and satisfied smiles as possible. Your satisfaction is our inspiration.
